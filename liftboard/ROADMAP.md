# Outlift roadmap

Live app: https://outliftt.netlify.app · Backend: Supabase project `liftboard` (zmchhnjwgyvyjixuwpuq)

## Done
- Accounts, splits (ready-made + custom), workout logger with rest timer
- Points leaderboard: active days, PRs, cardio, recovery, sets hit, variety, consistency, goals, team spirit; −5 per real lift drop, −20 per extra day off (4 rest days a week free)
- Cardio & recovery logging (run, swim, treadmill, StairMaster, sauna, ice bath …)
- Public by default with a private switch, groups with invite codes, local leaderboard
- Coach mode: group owners assign plans to members
- Exercise screens: demo video (mostly PureGym), real start/finish photos, steps, tips, equipment
- QR codes for gym machines, AI chat coach, AI recap, push notifications, badges, goals
- Owner password reset, compact phone-friendly layout
